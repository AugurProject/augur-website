## Key terms

The words you'll meet most often, in plain language.

- **Oracle.** A service that tells a blockchain what happened in the real world.
- **Report.** A proposed answer to a question, backed by a REP bond.
- **Challenge.** A different answer posted with a larger bond. It reopens the question.
- **Bond.** REP put at risk behind an answer. Wrong answers lose most of their bond to the winners.
- **Fork.** The last resort. REP holders move to the answer they believe; the total that moves decides the original oracle's answer.
- **REP.** Reputation: the token that secures Augur's oracle.
- **Universe.** A version of the ledger with its own REP. A fork creates one child universe per answer.
- **Invalid.** The result for a question that's malformed or can't be answered.
