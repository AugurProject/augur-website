> Ask the Augur team anything, or share a project and the answers it depends on.

This is a form page. The HTML page at `/contact/` has a form for sending a message to the Augur team; this Markdown copy lists what it asks for and the other ways to reach the team. You can't send a message from this file.

## Send us a message

Ask a question, or share a project and the answers it depends on.

The form asks for:

- Name (required, up to 100 characters)
- Email (required, up to 254 characters)
- Company / project (optional, up to 200 characters)
- Website (optional, an address starting with https:// or http://)
- Message (required, up to 5,000 characters)

Notice on the form: we use your name and email only to reply. Your message is sent to the Augur team by email and isn't stored on this website.

Review flag: if the form isn't connected to its mail service, the page says it can't send anything and gives the email address instead.
