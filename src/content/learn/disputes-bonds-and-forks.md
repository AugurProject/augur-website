## Disagreement raises the stakes

Anyone can back a different outcome with a larger REP bond. A completed challenge opens another appeal period. Disagreement follows the same public rules.

## The last resort

A fork creates outcome universes with one-way REP migration. One child universe is created for each possible answer, each with its own REP.

## The final answer

When an appeal period ends without a new challenge, the latest outcome becomes final. Applications can use that answer under their own settlement rules.
