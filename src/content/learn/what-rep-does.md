## Reporting and challenging are optional

Each one puts a bond at stake: being right pays, being wrong costs.

## Report

Back an answer with a REP bond. If no one challenges it, you get the bond back plus part of the query fee.

## Challenge

Post double the bond against a wrong answer. If yours holds, you also win most of theirs.

## Risk

If your answer is overturned, you lose the bond. Most of it goes to whoever was right.

Proposed rules for Augur Lituus, which is in development.
